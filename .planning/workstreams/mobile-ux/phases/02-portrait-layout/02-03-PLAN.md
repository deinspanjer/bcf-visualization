<flagged_assumptions>
**F-05 — MOBP-03 was unclassified by the edge probe; these are the concrete edges this plan holds itself to.** Planner-authored and asserted in `test_rail_scrub_zoom_aware`: the auto-pan percentage is clamped to `[0, (zoom - 1) * 100]` and is identically 0 at zoom 1; the inner fraction is clamped to `[0, 1]`; a viewport fraction of 0 resolves to word position 0 and a fraction of 1 resolves to `total_words` (note: the probe row phrased this as `totalWords - 1`, but the live `setWordPos` clamps to `total_words` inclusive — the live behaviour is what is asserted); word targets are produced by `Math.round`, so the mapping is stable to within one word.

**F-06 — pan recomputes per pointer move, so a drag at zoom > 1 is non-stationary.** `panOffsetForPlayhead` is fed the LIVE `app.wordPos`, which the drag itself is changing, exactly as the prototype does. The consequence is that at zoom > 1 a continuous drag's mapping shifts as the rail auto-pans under the finger. This is faithful to the approved prototype and is therefore the default; the alternative (freezing the pan offset at pointerdown for the duration of a drag, which makes a drag stationary but decouples it from the auto-pan animation) is on the Phase B gate agenda for Dre's feel check on real hardware. The automated tests assert single-press determinism precisely because the drag case is a feel judgement, not a numeric one.
</flagged_assumptions>
