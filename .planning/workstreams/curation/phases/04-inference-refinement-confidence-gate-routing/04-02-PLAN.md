<objective>
Add the agent-run ledger (CINF-04) that makes Stage 2 idempotent: a keyed, read-before-write
store recording, per chapter, the fingerprint of everything that could change its output (prose,
Stage 1 candidates, prompt/conventions version, model id), so re-running against unchanged
inputs costs nothing and produces byte-identical output, while a real input change forces
reprocessing rather than silently preserving stale output.

Purpose: This is the resumability backbone Plan 04-03's Batches-API multi-chapter runner and
Plans 04-04/04-05's calibration/held-out runs depend on — without it, every batch invocation
would blindly re-spend on chapters it already processed.
Output: `scripts/stage2_ledger.py` wired into `run_stage2_chapter.py`, plus a mocked-client
idempotency test proving both the no-diff-on-rerun and the fingerprint-invalidates-on-change
cases.
</objective>
