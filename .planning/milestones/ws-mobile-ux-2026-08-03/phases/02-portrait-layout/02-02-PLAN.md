<flagged_assumptions>
**F-03 — MOBP-02 was unclassified by the edge probe; these are the concrete edges this plan holds itself to.** The probe returned `unclassified` for MOBP-02 rather than a shape category, so no automatic acceptance criteria were derived. Planner-authored edges, all enforced by `web/mobile-gestures.js`'s constants and asserted in `test_sky_gesture_contract`: a press is a tap only within 250ms and 8px of movement; a second tap counts as a double-tap only within 300ms and 24px of the first; a drag engages as a swipe only past 24px of horizontal travel with horizontal movement at least 1.5x vertical; once engaged, one step fires per whole 56px traversed (a 140px drag = 24px engage + 2 x 56px = exactly 2 steps); the first tap of a double-tap pair fires `onTap` as well, by the contract's own ambiguity resolution. These constants are not re-implemented here — they are asserted through the module's observable behaviour.

**F-04 — speed multipliers versus the live words-per-second model.** The UI-SPEC's Speed row records the prototype's own state model (values `0.5`/`1`/`2`/`4`). The live `app.speed` is words per second (`tickPlayback` uses `app.wordPos + app.speed * dt`), so the labels are mapped onto rungs rather than written literally. The mapping chosen is 2500/5000/10000/25000 so no mobile-set value can render the frozen desktop `<select>` blank; the alternative (an exact 4x = 20000, which is not a desktop rung) is the trade-off for Dre at the gate.
</flagged_assumptions>
